Electronic Invoice Questions

Everything you need to know about the electronic invoice system and how businesses can use it efficiently.

What is an electronic invoice? +
An electronic invoice (e-invoice) is a digital invoice issued through an integrated system connected with the Egyptian Tax Authority. It replaces traditional paper invoices and allows invoices to be verified and archived electronically.
Why is the electronic invoicing system important? +
It helps reduce tax evasion, increases transparency, and automates invoice validation between companies and the tax authority.
Who must use electronic invoices? +
Companies registered with the Egyptian Tax Authority that perform B2B transactions must issue invoices electronically according to the phases announced by the government.
How can a company register in the electronic invoice system? +
Companies must register through the Egyptian Tax Authority portal, obtain a digital signature certificate, and integrate their accounting system with the e-invoice platform.
What are the benefits of electronic invoicing? +
Electronic invoices reduce paperwork, improve accuracy, speed up audits, and allow businesses to archive invoices securely.
Can electronic invoices be edited after submission? +
No. If an invoice needs correction, a credit note or debit note must be issued instead.
Is a digital signature required? +
Yes. A digital signature verifies the authenticity of the invoice before sending it to the tax authority system.
